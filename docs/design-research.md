# Finance Product Design Research

The product will use an original visual system informed by public finance-research interface patterns, rather than copying any third-party screen or branded treatment. The goal is a compact, legible workspace that helps users scan changing market data and start evidence-aware research with minimal navigation overhead.

| Reference pattern | Observed treatment | Original application decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Perplexity Finance | A search-led research surface paired with concise, source-oriented financial answers and market data | Use a centered research prompt on the home route, retain a persistent but restrained navigation bar, and keep AI answers and artifacts in a dedicated, resizable workspace. |
| Google Finance | A prominent instrument search field, compact regional market strip, data tables, price-direction color, and a side-by-side research panel | Use a compact market strip, clear search autocomplete, tabular gainers and movers, readable card grouping, and visual distinction between positive and negative price movement. |

The interface should favor a high-density desktop grid, a narrow visual palette, typography with strong numerical tabular alignment, 12–16 pixel radii, moderate shadows, and accessible contrast in both themes. Light and dark themes will share the same spacing and information hierarchy, while color will be reserved for price direction, interactive focus, and selected states.
